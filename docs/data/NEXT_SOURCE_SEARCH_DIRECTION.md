# Next Source Search Direction

## Objective

Find a business-directory-style source for a small manual first-100 candidate experiment. Do not use the Kyoto City construction-recycling notification dataset for candidate collection.

## Prefer

- Business or operator directories
- Registered-business lists
- Certified, approved, or designated-contractor lists
- Subsidy-recipient or adopted-business lists
- SME, industrial-support, and local-government business directories
- Open-data datasets with an explicit reuse license and business-entity fields

## Avoid as a First Choice

- Project-notification datasets and data with detailed work-site addresses or schedules
- Lists that prohibit commercial use or reuse
- Procurement systems limited to bidding use
- Association directories without reuse or storage permission
- Google Maps or Google Business Profile scraping

## Selection Criteria

A source is promising when it identifies businesses rather than projects, has explicit reuse terms or no relevant prohibition, avoids personal and sensitive project data, supports regional and industry filtering, can yield 10–30 businesses through manual review, and permits provenance and attribution to be recorded.
