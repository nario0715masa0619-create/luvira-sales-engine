# Manual Seed 10-Company Pilot

## Decision

Broad external candidate-source exploration is paused for the MVP. Earlier directories and permit lists had unclear or restrictive reuse conditions, while clearly licensed open data was mostly off-target. The immediate priority is validating the Sales Engine workflow, not finding a scalable source.

## Manual Seed List

A Manual Seed List is a small set of businesses individually selected by a human without copying or bulk-extracting a third-party directory. Valid origins include operator knowledge, referrals, normal business activity, ordinary public web search, human suggestions, and legitimate internal contacts. Scraped directories, copied association lists, Google Maps extraction, mass portal extraction, and purchased lists without provenance are excluded.

## Scope

Enter up to 10 human-supplied businesses in Hyogo, Osaka, Kyoto, or nearby Kansai. Prefer electrical, HVAC, plumbing, pipework, related specialist construction, and equipment businesses. Prefer no-website candidates; weak-website candidates are allowed. This is workflow validation, not market validation.

## Workflow

1. A human supplies or individually identifies a business and its provenance.
2. Create a candidate record with `discovery_source_type = Manual Seed List`.
3. Confirm basic identity using ordinary public information.
4. Follow the [No Website Search Procedure](../sales/NO_WEBSITE_SEARCH_PROCEDURE.md) and record evidence.
5. Record only verified public facts needed for sales preparation.
6. Prepare an offer hypothesis, sample-site concept, and outreach-message draft.
7. A human reviews facts, website status, sample content, contact permission, and wording before any outreach.

No automated discovery, extraction, or outreach is permitted.

## Safeguards and Success Criteria

Record provenance, date, and human operator for every entry. Do not invent facts, treat search absence as proof, or contact anyone without human approval. The pilot succeeds when 10 legitimate candidates have documented provenance and website review, at least 5 are suitable for sales-material preparation, preparation time can be measured, and reviewers can decide outreach readiness consistently.

## Next Decision

After the pilot, decide whether to proceed to human-approved outreach, refine the workflow, or resume scalable-source discovery.
