# Initial Target Segment

## Segment

**Hypothesis:** Start with equipment work / specialist construction companies:
electrical, HVAC, plumbing, fire equipment, communications, exterior,
demolition, painting, waterproofing, interior finishing, and maintenance or
cleaning.

## Rationale

**Hypothesis:** Associations, cooperatives, permits, and public local lists may
support API-independent discovery; website samples can be templated by
sub-industry; and improved inquiry flow may support an entry offer and upsells.

## Boundaries

**Decision:** Limit the first experiment to a defined local market, preferably
Kansai or nearby, and split results by subcategory. This is not a final ICP or
market decision.

## Open Questions

- Which prefecture, city, and subcategory have usable permitted sources?
- Are proposed 98,000–298,000 JPY entry prices economically viable?
