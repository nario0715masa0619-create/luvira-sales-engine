# First 100-Company Experiment

## Objective

**Decision:** Validate short-term sales opportunities and revenue from reviewed,
company-specific materials; do not optimize for a complete company database.

## Cohort and allocation

**Hypothesis:** Start in Kansai, prioritizing Hyogo, Osaka, and Kyoto. Allocate
35 electrical contractors, 35 HVAC/air-conditioning contractors, and 30
plumbing/pipework contractors. Target 70–80 No Website Candidates and 20–30
Weak Website Candidates.

**Decision:** If permitted sources cannot supply a reviewable cohort, first add
fire equipment, communication equipment, or building maintenance/cleaning;
then expand to Nara, Shiga, Wakayama, or Okayama. Any reduced evidence standard
must be labeled and routed to `Needs Human Review`.

## Practical workflow

1. Choose a permitted source from the [source shortlist](../data/CANDIDATE_SOURCE_SHORTLIST.md).
2. Capture a candidate as `Discovered`; record provenance and source-term review.
3. Research identity and website status using the [search procedure](../sales/NO_WEBSITE_SEARCH_PROCEDURE.md).
4. Use the [lifecycle](../data/CANDIDATE_STATUS_LIFECYCLE.md) to review, prepare,
   approve, and manually contact candidates.
5. Log outcomes using the [candidate schema](../data/FIRST_100_CANDIDATE_SCHEMA.md).

## Metrics

**Decision:** Track candidates discovered and researched; no-website, weak-site,
and rejected counts; materials generated; outreach-approved and contacted
candidates; replies; meetings; won/lost deals; average preparation time;
response, meeting, and win rates; revenue; and segment-level performance.

Rates use contacted candidates as the denominator unless a different denominator
is explicitly labeled. Revenue is confirmed paid order value only.

## Success interpretation

**Hypothesis:** At least 50 human-reviewable candidates and 50 outreach-ready
material sets make the experiment operationally useful. One meeting is a
meaningful validation signal; one paid order is strong validation. These are
learning thresholds, not performance guarantees.

## Open Questions

- What review capacity and follow-up window apply to the first batch?
- Which manual channels, offers, and prices are approved?
- Which sources pass the initial terms and provenance assessment?
