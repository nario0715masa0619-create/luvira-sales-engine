# MVP Experiment

## Goal

**Hypothesis:** Company-specific website samples and sales materials can create
replies, meetings, and revenue from no-website or weak-website businesses.

## Cohort

**Decision:** Identify 100 specialist construction/equipment-work companies in
a limited local market: 70–80 No Website Candidates and 20–30 Weak Website
Candidates.

## Flow

1. Acquire company names from a permitted non-website source.
2. Record source and provenance; research website status through public search.
3. Classify, record observable weakness signals, and human-review the record.
4. Prepare a fact-grounded sample and outreach draft for approved candidates.
5. Conduct the first batch manually or semi-manually after review.
6. Log replies, meetings, outcomes, and loss reasons.

## Measures

**Decision:** Track candidate count, reviewable count, material-ready count,
contacted count, replies, meetings, wins/losses, and loss reasons. One meeting
is meaningful success; one paid order is strong success.

## Open Questions

- What defines the initial batch size and review SLA?
- Which manual outreach channel is permitted and appropriate?
- What minimum evidence is needed for each website-status classification?
