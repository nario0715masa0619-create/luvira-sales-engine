# Product Definition

## Purpose

**Decision:** Luvira Sales Engine will become Luvira's sales front-end: discover
candidates, judge sales suitability, research public information, prepare a
proposal/sample, execute reviewed sales activity, and learn from outcomes.

## MVP

**Decision:** The initial MVP is a documentation-first, 100-company sales
validation experiment. It produces structured, reviewable candidate records and
company-specific preparation materials; it does not implement production
software or automate contact.

## Business objective

**Decision:** The MVP's primary business goal is real sales opportunities and
short-term revenue validation, not building a perfect company database.

## Value proposition

**Hypothesis:** A sales/inquiry-flow improvement package is more compelling than
generic "homepage production." Candidate entry offers include a simple page,
basic website, inquiry/LINE/booking flow, and later AI or workflow services.

## Guardrails

**Decision:** No invented facts, fake proof, mass outreach, prohibited-source
scraping, or contact without human review. Verified facts, generic templates,
and clearly marked assumptions/placeholders are the only basis for samples.

## Open Questions

- Which local market and sub-industry begin the experiment?
- Which offers, prices, and sales channels are viable?
- What review capacity is available for the first 100 records?
