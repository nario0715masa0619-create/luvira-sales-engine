# Outreach Policy

**Decision:** No automated email, form submission, messaging, or mass outreach
is permitted in this foundation or a later MVP without explicit approval.

## Preconditions

- Human review approved provenance, identity, website status, sample, and message.
- The company has no clear prohibition against sales contact through that route.
- The message states no invented company facts or unsupported outcomes.
- Assumptions in a sample are labeled or removed.

## Generation concept

**Decision:** Generate a draft website sample from verified public facts plus a
generic sub-industry template. Generate a concise outreach draft that references
only reviewed observations, offers a useful review/sample, and avoids pressure
or impact guarantees. A human selects, edits, and sends any message.
