# Human Review Workflow

1. **Source review:** confirm source provenance and permitted intended use.
2. **Identity review:** confirm the record refers to the intended company.
3. **Website review:** validate status and every stated weakness with URLs.
4. **Material review:** distinguish verified facts, generic template content,
   and assumptions/placeholders in every sample.
5. **Contact review:** check contact terms, opt-out/prohibition notices, tone,
   and manual outreach approval.
6. **Outcome review:** log factual result and source; do not reinterpret as ICP.

**Decision:** Any unresolved identity, provenance, prohibition, or factual claim
puts the record in `Needs Human Review` or `Manual Hold`. Approval is required
before outreach.
