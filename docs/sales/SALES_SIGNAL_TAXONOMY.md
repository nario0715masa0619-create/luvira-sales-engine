# Sales Signal Taxonomy

**Decision:** A sales signal is an observed, source-linked condition relevant to
preparing a sales conversation; it is not proof of buying intent.

| Signal | Evidence requirement |
| --- | --- |
| no_official_website_found | Search method, date, queries, reviewer conclusion |
| weak_website_observed | URL and specific observable issue |
| inquiry_flow_absent_or_unclear | URL/page and observation |
| SNS_or_portal_only_presence | URLs and reviewer conclusion |
| public_contact_route | Public source and contact-policy review |
| prohibited_sales_contact | Source/notice and exclusion reason |

**Decision:** Never label a company "likely to buy" as a sales signal. Outcomes
are logged separately and do not become ICP truth without later validation.
